# November 23, 2026 — Show sharing and ticket referrals

Artist teams can share a performance from their dashboard or its demand report. Fans can share it from the public show page. Each share uses the public key for the selected performance, including when two sets at the same venue share a date and slug. Existing links still resolve. A redirect to a canonical show address preserves validated campaign parameters.

Ticket offers distinguish artist-team, fan, legacy share and onsite referrals. An eligible Impact deep link can report the referral source, selected show and button placement when the approved US link is configured. Conversion and earnings reconciliation remain with the affiliate network. [Measurement details](../analytics.md#ticket-share-attribution)

The regression suite covers the actual share button and ticket anchor for both share origins, older links, duplicate show slugs and redirect attribution. The production release also updates the README, sync schedules and Google Search runbook. [Catalog and operations documentation](../README.md)
